# NOTES — Variant A: "J" for jetlagged

## Concept
Letter **J**, word **jetlagged**. Body clock wrecked at 4am without ever
travelling — an airport zombie in your own living room. Title:
"J for Jetlagged".

## Why this letter/word fits the 5 judge criteria
1. **First letter of a day-word:** J is the first letter of "jetlagged",
   a real word describing the day. It stands for something.
2. **Vivid and specific:** "jetlagged" calls up one sharp picture —
   bleary eyes, a body clock hours off, coffee at an hour coffee should
   not exist. Not a vague mood like "tired" or "amazing".
3. **Funny / surprising:** jet lag with no flight, no airport, no travel
   at all — the joke lands at once and is easy to repeat to a friend.
4. **True to a real day:** an honest report of a night actually spent
   awake building bounties, not a performance.
5. **Letter suits its word:** J is the only honest initial for
   "jetlagged". J's day-word space is tiny (jetlagged / jittery — both
   strong), so whatever word the judge infers behind the letter, it
   lands on a good one.

## Why J beats the other two variants
- vs W ("wired"): "wired" is strong, but its space also holds "wasted"
  and "wrecked" — a judge could infer a weaker or darker word. J's space

  a day; "jetlagged" describes *today* — a body off-schedule right now —
  which reads truer as a one-day diary entry.
